## I05 at a glance / I05 一览

The original author is probability theorist [Russell Lyons](https://math.indiana.edu/about/faculty/lyons-russell.html). He first proposed concavity of the full finite determinantal-process entropy as Conjecture 9.2 in the 2003 paper *Determinantal Probability Measures*, published in *Publications Mathématiques de l'IHÉS*: [journal page](https://pmihes.centre-mersenne.org/articles/10.1007/s10240-003-0016-0/) and [original PDF](https://www.numdam.org/item/10.1007/s10240-003-0016-0.pdf).

Lyons restated the problem as Conjecture 2.6 in his invited article for the 2014 International Congress of Mathematicians: [published PDF](https://rdlyons.pages.iu.edu/pdf/icm-pub.pdf) and [arXiv page](https://arxiv.org/abs/1406.2707).

This repository gives an exact five-dimensional counterexample to the conjecture as stated over complex Hilbert spaces. The midpoint is a rational real symmetric kernel, the violating direction is purely imaginary Hermitian of rank four, and the strict entropy inequality is certified by exact rational calculations:

The result does not settle the real-symmetric-only restriction, the minimum possible counterexample dimension, or the separate stationary Toeplitz entropy-rate conjecture.

Each result states its precise mathematical scope, reproducibility coverage, and unresolved limits. A verification verdict is not a claim of absolute novelty, priority, authorship, or endorsement by the ICM or its organizers.
